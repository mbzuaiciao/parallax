# Disney/Hulu pilot: economic-claim error audit

Audit date: November 5, 2026. Implements [GitHub Issue #2](https://github.com/mbzuaiciao/parallax/issues/2), read in full with its comment thread (no comments). Branch: `experiment/disney-hulu-baseten-ab`. No applicable `AGENTS.md` found in the repository, nested directories or checked ancestors.

**Finding:** Both completed responses preserve the principal purchase mechanics. Parallax supplies more stakeholder analysis and better separates measurement classes, but also adds unsupported assertions about equity value, cash-flow classification and legal-obligor identity. Baseline avoids most of those claims but overstates appraisal timing and withholds even conditional creditor analysis. This is an observed tradeoff in one pair, not proof that structured prompting causes hallucination or improves correctness.

## Scope, inputs and annotation rules

Audited only the visible `message.content` for `results.baseline` and `results.parallax` in the existing local [completed response file](../results/disney-hulu-baseten-ab-20261105-low-8192.json). Both ended with `stop`; the original [truncated attempt](../results/disney-hulu-baseten-ab-20261105.json) is historical context only. Used the unchanged [seven-excerpt evidence packet](disney-hulu-baseten-evidence.json), saved requests and [pilot report](disney-hulu-baseten-ab-results.md). No new retrieval, inference, prompt optimization or external adjudication. Model-internal reasoning is not used as evidence.

Each row quotes exact visible model text, identifies its claimed citations (including inherited context where necessary), and adjudicates against the supplied SEC packet. Quotes may be short fragments: read their stated qualifications and the full raw answer together. Repeated identical propositions are grouped; materially changed qualifications are separate rows. Tables cover payment, valuation/appraisal assumptions, legal parties and recourse, recognition, stakeholder consequences, uncertainty and double-counting. These are semantic annotations, not equal-weight test items; no totals, error rates or condition scores are calculated.

- **supported:** Directly stated in the packet, or a faithful restatement; evidence-gap claims are supported only as statements about the packet.
- **qualified/inference:** A defensible conditional implication or contextual shorthand requiring a stated boundary; not an established SEC fact. The rationale supplies missing qualifications and does not silently validate stronger wording.
- **unsupported:** Available evidence does not establish the assertion or its certainty. This does not mean it is false in the world.
- **contradicted:** Incompatible with an explicit supplied fact or formula under the same conditions. No unambiguous whole-response contradiction is established here; ambiguous shorthand and overstated certainty are not automatically contradictions.

This audit refines the earlier report: compression of the appraisal sequence is not alone an observed factual error, and Parallax’s gross/net heading is ambiguous in context, rather than an established wrong payment amount. Neither improvement in presentation nor length earns factual credit.

## Comparison and possible mechanisms

**Factual grounding and classification.** Both preserve the greater-of equity floor, initial offset, conditional top-up, tax sharing and dated redeemable-equity amount; neither adds those measures into a duplicated exposure total or calls the floor a maximum. Parallax expressly declines to label the commitment recognized debt. An incorrect debt classification is therefore **not** an observed failure. Parallax’s legal-obligor field is less disciplined than its later refusal to infer priority: the collective name fills a legal slot without precise obligor evidence. A plausible group label is not sufficient for parent-creditor claim analysis.

**Stakeholder coverage versus reliability.** Baseline anchors common-equity discussion in the disclosed earnings-attribution charge and stays largely silent on credit consequences. Its blanket “no conclusion” is too restrictive: the payment creates a defensible conditional resource-use channel even without a forecast. Parallax discusses that channel, tax-benefit retention and prior ownership exposure, but “further reduces equity value” moves from cash expenditure to a net valuation conclusion without establishing acquired benefits or funding costs. Lower cash is not by itself proof of shareholder wealth destruction; this is a reasoning boundary, not a counterclaim that the acquisition created value. The operating-cash-flow label and allocation of resources to parent debt service likewise lack the necessary cash-flow and recourse evidence.

**Shared or analogous weaknesses.** Both sometimes harden the conditional $8.61bn estimate in later discussion, despite initially preserving the unpaid-offset caveat. Both compress the appraisal averaging procedure, but its third-appraiser condition is recoverable from the surrounding sequence; no definite common appraisal error is established. Baseline alone turns the expected 2024 determination into a definite completion year. Parallax similarly says the amount is not estimable until 2024, which is a different unsupported inference about feasibility. Both retain the underlying expectation correctly elsewhere; those correct passages do not erase local overstatements.

**Unsupported assertions unique to Parallax in this pair.** The legal-obligor field, necessary equity-value reduction, early-estimation prohibition, hypothetical accounting-entry sequencing, “separate line from debt” attributed to E7, and operating-cash-flow/debt-service assertions are not made by baseline. Appropriate possible mechanism labels are: legal-obligor field → **pressure to fill an absent schema field / unsupported inference**; equity-value and tax-cash-flow conclusions → **stakeholder extrapolation**; estimation and sequencing → **unsupported inference**; separate-line claim → **citation mismatch**. These labels describe candidate explanations, not demonstrated causes. The packet does support legitimate conditional stakeholder analysis, so not every interpretation beyond verbatim filing text is an error.

**Did the structure encourage filling gaps?** The answer follows the requested structure and fills the obligor field with an unverified collective identity; it then supplies more economic detail than baseline, including unsupported stakeholder consequences. Those are observable facts. But Parallax also leaves financing and recourse uncertain and refuses to infer pari passu priority, so the evidence does not show indiscriminate completion of missing fields. Both conditions were asked the same stakeholder question; different prompt length, one sample, fixed order and model variability prevent attributing the disputed claims specifically to the obligation map, separation headings or stakeholder instruction. An unverified causal explanation must not become the next unsupported claim.

## One narrow follow-up hypothesis and stopping boundary

The observations justify **one provisional hypothesis**: when a structured prompt requests stakeholder consequences from a packet containing payment mechanics but no valuation of acquired benefits, the model may turn a defensible cash-use channel into an asserted net equity-value loss rather than preserving uncertainty. The strongest anchor is Parallax’s “further reduces equity value,” contrasted with baseline’s earnings-attribution-only discussion. A valid test would need to separate instruction structure from other prompt differences and stochastic variation; this two-answer audit does not do so. No intervention, new prompt, experiment or test has been implemented or run.

This is a single-case, unblinded assistant audit against curated historical excerpts, not independent financial/legal adjudication. Omitted documents could support a presently unsupported claim; none was consulted to rescue the model’s citations. No unambiguous contradicted classification or net performance superiority is required for the negative finding. Claim grouping and unequal answer coverage make a numerical ranking misleading.
