## 01 — Boeing credit guarantees

- **F1 [E1]** Boeing is the disclosed guarantor at group level. The guaranteed party can receive payment if the original lessee or debtor fails to pay or perform specified services. Outstanding guarantees expire through 2036.
- **F2 [E2]** USD 15 million is the disclosed December 31, 2024 aggregate maximum potential payment across the outstanding credit guarantees.
- **F3 [E2]** USD 14 million is the same-date carrying amount within accrued liabilities. It describes the same guarantees.
- **U [E1–E2]** A covered failure could create a cash call within the disclosed maximum, so a Boeing senior unsecured creditor should consider contingent liquidity demand alongside the booked liability.
- **Limits.** Beneficiary identities, exact legal entity and contract-specific recourse, payment incidence, expected cash loss, trigger probability and exact payment schedule are unresolved. A blank collateral column is insufficient to infer zero recourse. Reject a USD 29 million exposure, a USD 1 million expected unfunded loss, or a claim that either disclosed number is payable now.
- **Provenance.** Checked the 2024 columns and unit header of Note 15 against its explanatory text and the continuation on p. 92. The row is a portfolio of guarantees, and the cap is the disclosed aggregate maximum. [Boeing 2024 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12927/000001292725000015/ba-20241231.htm)

## 02 — Starbucks operating leases

- **F1 [E1]** Starbucks group owes direct operating lease payments to lessors over time. USD 11,942.3 million is the undiscounted schedule, including USD 1,808.5 million in fiscal 2025.
- **F2 [E1, E3]** At September 29, 2024, the USD 10,234.7 million liability equals the schedule less USD 1,707.6 million imputed interest. Balance-sheet components are USD 1,463.1 million current and USD 8,771.6 million long-term. Correct reconciliation suffices for this item.
- **F3 [E2]** USD 1,672.5 million is historical fiscal 2024 cash paid related to operating lease liabilities. The separate USD 1.6 billion disclosure concerns leases yet to commence.
- **U [E1–E3]** The fiscal 2025 cash bucket is pertinent to liquidity and debt-service capacity. Later rents continue to draw on cash, and signed future starts matter for forward exposure. The liability is useful as a dated measurement of the underlying schedule.
- **Limits.** Reject treating the full liability as cash due at once, adding the carrying amount to the undiscounted payments, treating fiscal 2024 cash paid as prospective, or treating uncommenced commitments as an additional current recognized balance. Lessor names, exact due days, covenant treatment, security and legal priority are unspecified. The schedule is scoped to direct obligations and excludes specified related expenses.
- **Provenance.** Reconciled Note 10 against Item 7's cash requirements row and footnote (1), and the two consolidated balance-sheet rows. The six buckets sum to the reported total. [Starbucks 2024 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/829224/000082922424000057/sbux-20240929.htm)

## 03 — Sempra Energy and the City of Los Angeles

- **F1 [E1]** Sempra Energy and the Sempra Utilities entered the separate City settlement on March 26, 2007. The agreed payment to resolve the City's Continental Forge claims was USD 8.5 million on April 25, 2007. This was a present commitment with a scheduled cash date as of signing.
- **F2 [E1]** The USD 8.5 million replaced the City's USD 12 million under the earlier settlement, which had been scheduled in eight annual installments.
- **F3 [E2]** The filing separately reports USD 8.5 million actually paid on April 25, 2007. Count the obligation and its fulfillment as one payment.
- **U [E1–E2]** The switch from installments to a single April payment moved the cash requirement into a near-term liquidity window for the Sempra group, all else equal. The excerpts support an actual April cash outflow and a lower nominal amount for this City component.
- **Limits.** The filing summary gives no entity-specific allocation of the payment, exclusive payer, funding source, carrying value for the separate City agreement, ranking or recovery effect. Reject treating the old and new amounts as additive, or projecting a USD 3.5 million economic gain or increase in available cash from the nominal difference alone.
- **Provenance.** Checked both SDG&E paragraphs against the same disclosure in Sempra Energy's later Form 10-Q for September 30, 2007 and the affiliated issuer's June 30 filing. This is a retrospective reconstruction of the obligation, with payment evidence explicitly identified as subsequent. [SDG&E June 2007 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/86521/000008652107000055/sdge2qtr07.htm), [Sempra September 2007 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1032208/000008652107000072/sre10q307.htm), [Pacific Enterprises and SoCalGas June 2007 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/92108/000008652107000056/pe_scg2qtr07.htm)

## 04 — Sanofi group's Blueprint Medicines tender offer

- **F1 [E1–E2]** Conditions were satisfied and Rothko accepted validly tendered, unwithdrawn shares on July 17, 2025. Rothko must pay or cause payment for those shares promptly and within one business day after acceptance, with Sanofi obligated to cause Rothko to do so. Cash consideration is USD 129 per share before applicable withholding, with no interest.
- **F2 [E1, E3]** Eligible shares also receive one CVR each. The two separate milestone amounts are USD 2 and USD 4 per CVR, with a combined cap of USD 6. Credit this item only if the answer preserves the clinical-dosing and regulatory-approval triggers and their distinct deadlines, December 31, 2028 and June 30, 2032 respectively.
- **F3 [E1–E3]** Aventis, called Parent in the CVR contract, handles milestone deposits through the rights agent for the scoped holders. Each contingent payment depends on its own milestone. The defined payment dates follow determination of amounts after milestone achievement.
- **U [E1–E3]** Sanofi's undertaking to cause payment makes the fixed tender consideration an immediate group funding consideration. CVRs add a separate contingent future cash demand. The packet leaves the impact on standalone Sanofi resources and credit metrics unresolved.
- **Limits.** Reject USD 135 per share currently due, a guaranteed USD 6 payout, an established probability or booked fair value, and a claim that the rights agent owes the money from its own resources. Sanofi is Parent in the merger agreement while Aventis is Parent in the CVR agreement. Share count, total tender cash, actual disbursement, funding source and creditor ranking are outside this excerpt scope. The completed merger and non-tendered shares are outside the question.
- **Provenance.** Matched the 8-K's consideration and acceptance statements to §1.1(c) of the signed agreement and reconciled the USD 6 maximum with the two amounts and deposit rules in the executed CVR agreement.
