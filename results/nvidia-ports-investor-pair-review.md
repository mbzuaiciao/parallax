## Unsupported assumptions and precision errors

- **Equity: zero-cost inference.** The statement that tenant performance for twenty
  years makes cost zero is not established. The pack mentions a redacted pre-RFS
  payment and unspecified project/remedy costs, and supplies no complete cost
  economics. Its categorical no-payment statement also overextends conditions
  applying to Covered Loss Amount into a statement about every possible payment.
  This is an unsupported numerical/economic assertion, not a verified zero.
- **Equity: scope and labels.** Calling all downside capped before recoveries is
  too broad given acknowledged cap qualifications/non-reducing costs. Tenant
  Parent remains grouped under direct obligations despite its separate guarantee
  role and unspecified payment conditions. Claimed lost flexibility from exclusivity
  is an interpretation, not a disclosed cost borne by NVIDIA. Signing-time
  non-effectiveness is inferred from future commencement, not directly confirmed
  by a supplied commencement-status record.
- **Creditor: accounting inference.** Its fact-labeled claim that any payment increases
  liabilities is unsupported. A payment can settle an already recognized liability;
  the pack supplies no accounting recognition basis. Cash outflow is the supported
  point. Its final maximum-exposure wording must also retain the disclosed cap's
  qualifications rather than bound every possible cost.
- **Both: quantitative restraint.** Neither calculates leverage, expected loss or
  creditor recovery. Reproduced disclosed amounts are consistent; no numerical
  risk ground truth is available to score.
